🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING:

   No green button?
   → Clear cache, refresh page (Ctrl+Shift+R)
   
   Microphone not working?
   → Use Chrome browser, allow permission
   
   No sensor data?
   → Backend must be running, user must be logged in
   
   Rate limit error?
   → Wait 1 minute (10 requests/min limit)

📱 MOBILE:
