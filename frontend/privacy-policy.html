Introduction

Welcome to Krishi ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our smart farming platform.

📌 Summary: We collect only the information necessary to provide you with our farming services. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, farm location
  • Farm Data: Soil sensor readings, crop information, field boundaries
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform, features used
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial or health data.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Provide, maintain, and improve our smart farming services
  • Personalization: Tailor recommendations and insights for your farm
  • Communication: Send you updates, alerts, and important notifications
  • Analytics: Analyze usage patterns to improve our platform
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
💡 Note: We process your farm data to provide AI-powered insights. Your data helps us make better recommendations for your crops.

Data Sharing

We value your privacy and do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following cases:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate our platform
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you give us explicit permission

We require all third-party service providers to maintain the confidentiality of your data.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Control: Strict access controls and authentication protocols
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review our security practices
  • Secure Servers: Data is stored on secure, industry-standard servers
🔒 Security Commitment: We use 256-bit encryption and follow best practices to keep your farm data safe.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our platform. Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and login status
  • Analyze how you use our platform
  • Provide personalized recommendations
  • Improve our services based on usage patterns

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise your rights, please contact us at support@krishi.com.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services. We may retain certain information for legal compliance, fraud prevention, and service improvement purposes.

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Data retained for up to 12 months after account closure
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained for legal compliance

Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.

GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We ensure that:

  • You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data
  • We obtain explicit consent for data collection
  • We implement data protection by design and by default
  • We report data breaches within 72 hours
