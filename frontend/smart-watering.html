FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions

How does the system know when to water?
The system uses soil moisture sensors placed at root depth. When moisture drops below the optimal range for your crop, the system automatically starts irrigation. It stops when moisture reaches the ideal level.
What is the optimal moisture for my crop?
Different crops have different requirements. For wheat: 45-65%, rice: 70-85%, corn: 50-70%, vegetables: 60-80%. The system adjusts recommendations based on your selected crop.
Why is soil moisture important for crop growth?
Soil moisture dissolves nutrients and makes them available to plants. Without proper moisture, roots cannot absorb nutrients, leading to stunted growth and reduced yield.
What happens if moisture is too high?
Excess moisture causes waterlogging, which can lead to root rot, nutrient loss, and reduced oxygen availability for roots. The system stops irrigation when moisture is optimal.
Can I use this system for multiple crops?
Yes! You can set different moisture thresholds for each field and crop type. The system manages multiple fields simultaneously.
