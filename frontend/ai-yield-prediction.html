FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions

How accurate is the yield prediction?
Our AI models achieve over 90% accuracy in predicting yields. The accuracy improves as more data is collected from your farm. Factors like weather, soil conditions, and crop health are all considered.
What factors does the AI consider for prediction?
The AI analyzes soil moisture, temperature, pH, EC, historical yield data, weather patterns, crop variety, growth stage, and nutrient levels to make accurate predictions.
How can I improve my yield prediction?
Follow the recommendations provided by the system: optimize irrigation, apply balanced fertilizers, monitor pests regularly, and maintain optimal soil health. These actions will help maximize your yield.
When should I harvest based on the prediction?
The system predicts the harvest date based on crop growth stage and weather conditions. You'll receive a notification when your crop is ready for harvest. Generally, wheat is ready 18-22 days after flowering.
