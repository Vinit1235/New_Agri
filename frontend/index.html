Soil Parameters

Understanding Soil Health

Detailed information about each soil parameter and why it is critical for your crop production.

🧪 Nitrogen (N)
Optimal: 280-560 kg/ha
What it is: Nitrogen is the most important nutrient for plant growth. It is the building block of proteins, enzymes, and chlorophyll - the green pigment that helps plants make food through photosynthesis.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Without enough nitrogen, plants cannot produce food, leading to:
  • Pale yellow leaves (chlorosis) – sign of deficiency
  • Stunted growth and low yield
  • Poor grain quality and low protein content
  • Weak stems that break easily in wind
✅ Good: Dark green leaves ❌ Bad: Yellow leaves ✅ Good: Strong stems ❌ Bad: Weak stems ✅ Good: High protein grains ❌ Bad: Low quality grains
🧪 Phosphorus (P)
Optimal: 20-40 kg/ha
What it is: Phosphorus is essential for energy transfer in plants. It helps convert sunlight into food and plays a critical role in root development, flowering, and seed formation.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Phosphorus deficiency causes:
  • Poor root system – plants cannot absorb water
  • Delayed flowering and poor fruit set
  • Purple or dark green leaves (stress sign)
  • Low seed production and poor quality
✅ Good: Strong roots ❌ Bad: Weak roots ✅ Good: Better flowers ❌ Bad: No flowers ✅ Good: Faster maturity ❌ Bad: Delayed maturity
🧪 Potassium (K)
Optimal: 120-180 kg/ha
What it is: Potassium regulates water movement in plants and activates many enzymes. It helps plants withstand drought, disease, and extreme temperatures.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Potassium deficiency leads to:
  • Poor water efficiency – plants wilt easily
  • Weak stalks and stems that fall over
  • Increased disease and pest attacks
  • Poor grain filling and low quality
✅ Good: Water efficiency ❌ Bad: Wilting ✅ Good: Disease resistance ❌ Bad: Disease prone ✅ Good: Strong stems ❌ Bad: Lodging (falling)
🌡️ Temperature (T)
Optimal: 20-32°C
What it is: Soil temperature affects seed germination, root growth, and nutrient availability. Different crops have different temperature requirements for optimal growth.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Wrong temperature causes:
  • Poor seed germination – seeds rot in cold soil
  • Slow root growth and nutrient deficiency
  • Heat stress – leaves burn and wilt
  • Reduced crop yield and quality
✅ Good: Fast germination ❌ Bad: Poor germination ✅ Good: Healthy roots ❌ Bad: Stunted roots ✅ Good: Good nutrient uptake ❌ Bad: Nutrient lockup
🧪 Soil pH (pH)
Optimal: 6.0-7.5
What it is: pH measures how acidic or alkaline your soil is. It affects how well plants can absorb nutrients from the soil. Most crops grow best in slightly acidic to neutral soil.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Wrong pH causes:
  • Nutrient lock-up – plants starve even with fertilizer
  • Toxic aluminum and manganese in acidic soil
  • Poor beneficial bacteria activity
  • Reduced fertilizer efficiency – waste of money
✅ Good: Max nutrients ❌ Bad: Nutrient lock-up ✅ Good: Healthy microbes ❌ Bad: Poor microbes ✅ Good: Better fertilizer ❌ Bad: Waste fertilizer
💧 Soil Moisture (H₂O)
Optimal: 30-70%
What it is: Soil moisture is the water content present in your soil. It is essential for dissolving nutrients and making them available to plants through their roots.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Improper moisture causes:
  • Dry soil – roots cannot absorb nutrients
  • Waterlogged soil – roots rot and die
  • Plant wilting and stunted growth
  • Poor crop yield and quality
✅ Good: Nutrients dissolved ❌ Bad: No nutrients ✅ Good: Healthy roots ❌ Bad: Root rot ✅ Good: Better yield ❌ Bad: Low yield
⚡ EC (Salinity) (EC)
Safe: 0.5-2.5 dS/m
What it is: Electrical Conductivity measures the salt content in your soil. High salt levels can harm plants by making it difficult for roots to absorb water and nutrients.
🔴 Why It's Critical: High salinity causes:
  • Roots cannot absorb water – plant dehydrates
  • Leaf burn and yellowing
  • Stunted growth and reduced yield
  • Death of plants in severe cases
✅ Good: Healthy water uptake ❌ Bad: No water uptake ✅ Good: No salt stress ❌ Bad: Salt stress ✅ Good: Root protection ❌ Bad: Root damage
🌱 NPK Balance (N-P-K)
Ideal Balance: 4:2:1
What it is: NPK represents the three main nutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K). The right balance is crucial for maximum crop production.
🔴 Why It's Critical: Imbalance causes:
  • Too much nitrogen – excessive leaves, no fruit
  • Too little phosphorus – poor roots and flowering
  • Too little potassium – weak plants, disease
  • Overall poor yield and quality
✅ Good: Balanced growth ❌ Bad: Unbalanced growth ✅ Good: Max yield ❌ Bad: Low yield ✅ Good: Better quality ❌ Bad: Poor quality

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Krishi.

What is Krishi and how does it help farmers?
Krishi is a smart farming platform that helps Indian farmers monitor their crops, save water, reduce fertilizer costs, and get better yields. It uses sensors and AI to give you real-time information about your farm.
Do I need technical knowledge to use Krishi?
Not at all! Krishi is designed to be simple and easy to use for all farmers. Everything is explained in simple language and you can get started with basic training.
What sensors do I need to install on my farm?
You'll need soil moisture, temperature, pH, and EC sensors. Krishi provides complete sensor kits that are easy to install in your fields.
How accurate are the predictions?
Our AI models are over 90% accurate in predicting yields and giving recommendations. The accuracy improves as more data is collected from your farm.
Can I use Krishi without internet?
Yes! The sensors work fully offline, making irrigation and fertilizer decisions locally. When internet is available, data syncs to your dashboard automatically.
